Microsoft Hyper‑V continues to gain traction as organizations diversify beyond VMware and expand hybrid cloud deployments, and so does the attack surface that MSPs need to defend. The Arcserve ShadowProtect 8.0 release is purpose-built for modern Microsoft Hyper‑V environments with a host-based version. It's available now alongside agent-based backup; the general-purpose protection method MSPs already rely on across their environments (Hyper-V VMs included).
